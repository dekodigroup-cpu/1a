## Stop Paying the Patchwork Tax

You bought a website from one company, ads from another, a review tool from a third, and a customer list that nobody updates. Each one was sold as the answer. None of them talk to each other.

That's not a failure on your part. It's what happens when there are thousands of options and everyone is selling their piece. But it comes with a hidden cost:

- **Too many choices.** There are 15,384 marketing tools on the market. (chiefmartec, 2025)
- **Tools you pay for and barely use.** Marketing teams use only about a third of what their tools can do. (Gartner, 2023)
- **Time lost switching.** Workers jump between apps about 1,200 times a day and lose nearly 4 hours a week getting refocused. (Harvard Business Review, 2022)
- **No idea what's working.** 73% of small businesses lack confidence in their marketing strategy. (Constant Contact, 2024)
- **Leads that go cold.** Companies that reply within an hour are nearly 7 times as likely to reach a decision maker as those that wait longer. (Harvard Business Review, 2011)

There's a well-known study about this. Shoppers shown 24 kinds of jam mostly walked away without buying. Shoppers shown 6 bought at about ten times the rate. (Iyengar and Lepper, 2000) More options get attention. Clear options get decisions.
