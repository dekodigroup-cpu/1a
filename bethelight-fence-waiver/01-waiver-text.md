**Organization:** Be The Light Rescue `[full legal name, e.g. "Be The Light Rescue, Inc., a [STATE] nonprofit corporation"]` ("**Be The Light**")

**Signer** (the person completing this form): ____________________ ("**I**", "**me**", "**Property Representative**")

## 1. Purpose

A lost, stray, or loose animal (the "**Animal**") is on or near the Property. To capture it safely, Be The Light may need to enter the Property, set up equipment, and **work on, around, or through fences, gates, and other structures**. I am signing this form to give that permission and to release Be The Light from liability, as described below.

## 2. My Authority Over the Property

I confirm that I am **one** of the following:

- the owner of the Property;
- a tenant, property manager, or agent who has the legal right to grant access **and**, where required, the owner's or landlord's permission to do so.

If I am not the owner, I have given the owner's/landlord's contact information below, and I accept responsibility for any claim the owner or landlord brings that results from my granting this permission.

## 3. Permission to Enter

I grant Be The Light, and its directors, officers, employees, volunteers, foster caregivers, contractors, and agents (together, the "**Rescue Parties**"), permission to enter all outdoor areas of the Property — including yards, fenced areas, driveways, sheds, outbuildings, under decks and porches, and crawl-space openings — **at any time of day or night**, with or without advance notice, during the Authorization Period (Section 9), for the purpose of locating, monitoring, luring, trapping, and securing the Animal.

Entry into the inside of a house or occupied dwelling requires separate, specific permission from me at the time.

## 4. Authorized Work — Fences, Gates, and Equipment

I authorize the Rescue Parties to do whatever they reasonably believe is necessary to secure the Animal, including any of the following that I have checked on the form:

1. **Existing fences and gates:** climb over, open, prop, unlatch, lift, or remove fence panels, pickets, boards, or gates; and, if reasonably needed, **cut, bend, or detach** wire, chain-link, mesh, or fasteners.
2. **Temporary fencing:** install temporary fencing, catch-pens, corrals, funnels, or panels, including driving stakes or posts into the ground.
3. **Blocking escape routes:** block, fill, or cover gaps, holes, and openings under or through fences, decks, and structures.
4. **Digging and ground work:** dig, move soil or rocks, or remove debris to reach or contain the Animal.
5. **Vegetation:** trim or move brush, shrubs, or plants that block access.
6. **Traps and equipment:** set and leave humane traps, drop traps, feeding stations, trail/security cameras, signage, and related equipment on the Property, and return to check, bait, and remove them.
7. **Food and attractants:** place food, water, and scent lures on the Property.
8. **Vehicles:** park on the Property and drive on driveways and reasonable paths to reach equipment.

The Rescue Parties will use reasonable efforts to **restore or close** fences and gates when the work is done, but **they are not required to repair, replace, or pay for** anything changed or damaged during the work.

## 5. Assumption of Risk

I understand that this work can result in damage to fences, gates, lawns, landscaping, structures, and other property; that animals that are frightened may bite, scratch, or escape; that my own pets or livestock could get out through an opened fence or gate; and that food and traps may attract other wildlife. **I knowingly and voluntarily accept all of these risks**, whether known or unknown, present or future.

I am responsible for securing my own animals, children, and guests while work is underway, and for telling Be The Light about any hazards I know of (Section 8).

## 6. Release and Waiver of All Claims

**To the fullest extent permitted by law**, I, for myself and for my heirs, family members, tenants, landlord, insurers, successors, and assigns, **release, waive, discharge, and forever give up any and all claims, demands, and causes of action** against the Rescue Parties, of any kind, **whether known or unknown, existing now or arising in the future**, for:

- damage to, destruction of, or loss of value of the Property, including fences, gates, structures, lawns, landscaping, utilities, and personal property;
- the escape, loss, injury, or death of any animal, including my own pets or livestock and the Animal itself;
- bodily injury to me or anyone on the Property; and
- any other loss, cost, or expense,

**arising out of or related to** the Rescue Parties' presence on the Property or the work described in this form, **including claims based on the ordinary negligence of the Rescue Parties**.

This release does **not** apply to claims arising from gross negligence or intentional misconduct, to the extent the law does not allow those to be waived.

## 7. Covenant Not to Sue and Indemnification

I agree **not to sue** any of the Rescue Parties for any claim released in Section 6.

I agree to **defend, indemnify, and hold harmless** the Rescue Parties from any claim, demand, lawsuit, cost, or attorney's fees brought by **any other person** — including an owner, landlord, tenant, HOA, neighbor, guest, or my insurer — that arises from my granting this permission or from work done on the Property under it.

## 8. Information I Have Provided

The hazard, access, and pet information I give on this form is true to the best of my knowledge. This includes buried utilities, irrigation lines, invisible/electric fences, pools, wells, aggressive animals, chemicals, and gate codes. Be The Light may rely on it.

## 9. Authorization Period and Revocation

This permission starts when I sign and continues **until the Animal is secured and all equipment is removed, or until the end date I list on the form, whichever comes first**. I may revoke it at any time by notifying Be The Light **in writing** (email or text is enough). Revocation does not affect the release for anything that happened before it.

## 10. Photos and Video

I consent to the Rescue Parties placing cameras on the Property to monitor the Animal and to photographing or filming the Animal and the rescue. Be The Light may use these images for rescue, reunification, and nonprofit outreach purposes. Be The Light will not intentionally publish my name, street address, or images of people on the Property without my permission.

## 11. No Ownership Claim; No Fee

Signing this form does not give me any ownership right in the Animal, and it does not make Be The Light responsible for any veterinary bill, reward, or cost related to the Animal unless separately agreed in writing. Be The Light provides this service as a volunteer nonprofit and charges no fee for it.

## 12. General Terms

- **Severability:** If any part of this form is found unenforceable, the rest stays in effect, and the unenforceable part will be enforced to the greatest extent the law allows.
- **Governing law:** This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of `[STATE]`.
- **Entire agreement:** This form is the full agreement on this subject and can be changed only in writing signed by both me and Be The Light.
- **Electronic signature:** I agree that my typed or drawn electronic signature on this form is legally binding, the same as a handwritten signature, under the federal E-SIGN Act and the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act.
